Be patient and gentle with yourself — there's no rush, and nobody is keeping score. Rest, heal, and lean on the people around you.

We are here for you — for anything at all, big or small. And we'll be waiting, with a table saved, for the day you're ready.

With so much love and every good wish, Pun and Palm (+ Palin)
